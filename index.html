Odds shown are anytime-TD-scorer prices from DraftKings and FanDuel, refreshed automatically once a day via a scheduled job, plus an optional manual refresh (Wed–Sun 1pm ET, a few times a day) — not fetched once when this page was built. They're for reference when choosing a player and are locked in at pick time; the sportsbook's live line may move after that. Fantasy team names are pulled live from the league's ESPN page. Picks, team locks, and other entries are all stored in a database that only allows new entries to be created — nobody (including us) can edit or delete one once it's submitted, so the record can't be vandalized or quietly changed.
